From one skill to the whole workflow.

Test whether AI gets the job done, stays within its limits and knows when to stop. The task sets the rules, not the model brand.

Skills and instructions
Does the agent follow the instructions, produce the required work and handle unusual requests?
Agent loops
Does it make progress, recover from errors and stop instead of repeating or spending without a limit?
Workflows and teams of agents
Do tool calls and handoffs produce the right result while respecting permissions and human review?
The system around the agent
Do required checks run, records stay intact and release rules stop work that is not ready?

Published examples currently focus on skills and the platform's own checks. Testing your workflow means connecting its runner and recording the steps you want checked. See current tooling and integration limits.
